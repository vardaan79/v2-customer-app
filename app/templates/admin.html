Where this data lives

Everything on this panel is stored in Snowflake, not on this machine — V2RETAIL.CDP_WORK.APP_OFFER, APP_OFFER_STORE, APP_STORE_LOCATION, APP_NUDGE and APP_NUDGE_READ. Customer profile details go to CUSTOMER_PROFILE. Any other app or report reading those tables sees the same content.
